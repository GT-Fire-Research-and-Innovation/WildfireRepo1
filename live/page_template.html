Wildfire Spread Forecast

Where a fire is likely to burn next, from the project's physics-informed model, with the crown-fire and ember maps behind it. Replay shows a past forecast against what really burned.

How this forecast is made

Rothermel surface spread with crown fire (Cruz et al. 2004–2005) and ember spotting, run on a 150 m grid by a travel-time solver. Weather is forecast-only: HRRR observations up to the start time plus HRRR forecasts already issued then. Fuels from LANDFIRE, terrain from Copernicus GLO-30, fire from WFIGS perimeters and VIIRS detections. Prototype: not for operational decisions.
